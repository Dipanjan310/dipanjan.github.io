Publications

Submitted to IEEE TCOM2026

Performance Analysis of Multiparty GHZ-State Quantum Conference Key Agreement over Optical Fiber Channel

PhD research · First author

Analyzes an N-party GHZ-state quantum conference key agreement protocol using the N-BB84 scheme over optical-fiber channels. Models fiber attenuation, polarization-mode-dispersion-induced dephasing, and detector dark counts; derives bit- and phase-error rates and incorporates them into finite-key secret-key-rate analysis.

arXiv:2610.05448 ↗

Accepted at IEEE ANTS 20262026

Redundant Routing Protocol for Key Rate Maximization in QKD Networks

PhD research · Co-author

Studies key-rate maximization for multiple source–destination pairs in multi-hop QKD networks with trusted quantum repeaters. Develops a redundant-routing framework using Key Distribution Link Establishment (KDLE), formulated as a linear program, and Key Distribution Path Selection (KDPS), evaluated through E91-based simulations.
