ABOUT ME

Researching secure quantum communication networks.

I am a PhD research scholar in Applied Electronics at IIT Delhi. My research interests include quantum key distribution (QKD), quantum communication, quantum networks, and quantum information processing.

My current work explores efficient key distribution and multiparty quantum conference key agreement, with an emphasis on communication over optical-fiber channels and practical network performance.
